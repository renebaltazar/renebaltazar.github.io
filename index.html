About

I am a professor of Mathematics at the Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG), Brazil.

My research is mainly in Algebra, with emphasis on noncommutative algebra, derivations and automorphisms of algebras.

Research Interests

Noncommutative Algebra · Derivations · Automorphisms · Isotropy Groups · Quantum Algebras · Ore Extensions · Danielewski-type Algebras
