Rene Baltazar

My research interests lie in Commutative Algebra and Algebraic Geometry, especially in derivations, automorphisms, and isotropy groups. More recently, I have been exploring these questions in noncommutative settings, particularly for Ore extensions and related algebras.

Associate Professor of Mathematics
Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG), Brazil

Ph.D. advisor: I. Pan (UdelaR).
M.Sc. advisor: L. Doering (UFRGS).

Email: renebaltazar.furg@gmail.com
